    How to export a chat from WhatsApp
    1. Open the chat and tap its name at the top.
    2. Scroll down and tap Export Chat.
    3. Pick Attach Media to keep photos, videos and voice notes.
    4. Save the .zip to Files (or email it to yourself).
    5. Click Import here, or drop the .zip anywhere on this page.
    Chats are read in your browser and kept only on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
